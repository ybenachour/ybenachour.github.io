01 / About

From photonics
to applied AI.

An interdisciplinary researcher and educator with more than fifteen years of experience across applied physics, data science and artificial intelligence.

My work focuses on machine learning for health and human movement, explainable and privacy-preserving AI, and intelligent engineering systems. I combine computational modeling with practical applications, drawing on experience in higher education, energy and financial services.

At the Higher Colleges of Technology, I teach, develop curricula and supervise student research in AI, data science and engineering. My doctoral work at Université Paris 11 Orsay explored the optimization of photonic crystals for nonlinear optics.
